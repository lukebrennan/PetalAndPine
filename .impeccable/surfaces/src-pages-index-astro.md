# Home page (and site-wide shell)

Scope: Persuade. Visitor has a person and an occasion in mind; they should know what the shop makes, what it costs from, and how to get in touch within one screen. Primary action: "Plan a bouquet" (the contact-page brief builder). Site is a demo; one discreet notice in footer and contact page only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the florist's walled-garden glasshouse: a whitewashed glazing grid, bottle-green paint owning whole sections, and every bouquet carrying a slate label with its name and price. It refuses the cream-ground, italic-serif, eyebrow-and-card florist template.

OWN-WORLD: Limewash ground #f2f3ee; bottle green #14422f owns the hero band, the footer and one mid-page section; terracotta #c4603a is the only action colour; slate #3b4248 labels with chalk-white type carry names and prices; moss #b9c7b3 hairlines are the glazing bars that rule the grid. Young Serif for display, Karla for everything else; no italics, no eyebrows, no numbering. Square corners everywhere.

STORY: "A Chester florist that makes loose garden-style bouquets in three palettes from £30; pick a palette and size, write a brief, send it." The visitor believes it is a real small shop and leaves with a brief.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header: wordmark left, nav, terracotta "Plan a bouquet" right. Below, a two-pane glasshouse: left pane on limewash holds "Petal & Pine" at display scale with "Florist in Chester" beneath and two lines of plain copy; right pane is the bouquet photograph bleeding to the viewport edge. A bottle-green band interlocks under the photo and carries three slate labels (palette name, from £, one line), each a link.

FORM: Victorian walled-garden glasshouse, position 3 of 7 on the grounded list. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
